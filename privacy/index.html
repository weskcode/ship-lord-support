Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Ship Lord helps you learn about building and shipping apps. This policy explains how the app and these support pages handle information.

Your learning data stays on your device

Ship Lord stores your lesson progress, review history, practice activity, Ship Log notes and settings in a local database. It uses that information to show your progress and schedule practice.

The app works without an account. It does not automatically upload your learning data to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising or app-integrated analytics service.

Backups you choose to share

When you choose to share a backup, Ship Lord sends a copy of your learning data, notes and settings to the system share sheet. You choose its destination. That service or recipient can access the copy under its own practices.

Backups can contain personal notes. Store them somewhere you trust and avoid posting them publicly. Restoring a backup replaces the progress on that installation after you confirm.

Your device's system backup settings may also include app data. Those backups are managed by your operating system and backup provider; they are separate from Ship Lord's manual backup feature.

Keeping and deleting data

Local learning data remains on your device while you keep the app and its data. On iPhone, choosing Delete App removes the app's local data. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Copies you exported or kept in system backups must be managed separately with their destination or backup provider.

Links and support

Optional learning links open other websites. Those sites handle visitor information under their own policies.

These pages and the public support issue tracker are hosted by GitHub, which may process visitor and account information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you open a support issue, your GitHub username and report are public. Do not include backup contents, personal notes or credentials.

Questions and updates

Ship Lord's developer maintains the public support tracker. You can ask a general privacy question there without sharing personal information. See Support for reporting guidance.

This policy will be updated when the app's practices change. The date above identifies this version.
