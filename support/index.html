Ship Lord support

Help with lessons, progress and backups.

Report a problem

Open a support request on GitHub. A GitHub account is required. You can also read existing reports.

Reports are public. Do not post your backup, personal Ship Log notes, passwords or other private information.

Include these details so the problem can be reproduced:

  • Your app version from Settings → About.
  • Your phone model and operating-system version.
  • What you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
  • A screenshot with personal information removed, if it helps.

Keep a copy of your progress

Open Settings → Backup and restore → Back up your progress. Then tap Share a backup. On iPhone, use Save to Files, Notes or Copy in the share sheet. Keep a copy somewhere that is not only on this phone, and check that it arrived before relying on it.

Closing the share sheet can cancel the export. Ship Lord does not automatically sync progress between devices.

On Android, tap Yes, I saved it only after checking the destination. Dismissing its share sheet can look like success to the system. If the app reports that a backup is too large to share, no backup was recorded and your progress is unchanged.

Restore carefully

Open Settings → Backup and restore → Back up your progress to reach the restore field. Paste the complete backup and choose Check backup. Read the summaries of both the backup and your current progress before confirming. Replacing progress is permanent and cannot be undone. It overwrites the current installation's learning data, including notes and settings; an empty backup erases that progress.

Save a current backup before replacing progress. If a backup is rejected, your progress stays unchanged; check that the entire original text was copied. Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step unless you have a usable backup and intend to remove its local data.

Lessons and outside resources

The early lessons work on your phone. Some real-world tasks need a computer or an account with another service. External sites, requirements and tools can change. Include the unit and lesson title when reporting a content error.
